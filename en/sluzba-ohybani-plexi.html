Acrylic bending and bonding

Acrylic bending and bonding

We specialise in processing plastics, especially plexiglass and polycarbonate. We create functional and decorative products tailored to your requirements – from simple holders to complex POS/POP display stands.

Applications

  • Brochure stands
  • Machine guards
  • Display cases
  • Boxes
  • Counters
  • Design accessories
  • Illuminated signs

What we offer

  • Hot bending: Precise bending at any angle without damaging the material.
  • Invisible, strong bonding: Professional solvent bonding with a clean finish – no bubbles and no visible traces of adhesive.
