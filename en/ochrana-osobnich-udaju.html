Privacy policy

How we handle your data and which cookies the website uses.

Who processes your data

The controller of your personal data is INVAKO servis s.r.o., company ID 01783564, registered office Podnásepní 450/1a, 602 00 Brno, Czech Republic.

For any data protection matter you can contact us at info@invako.cz or by phone at +420 775 565 674.

What data we process

When you send us a request through the form or contact us by e-mail or phone, we process the details you give us yourself:

  • your name and, if applicable, your company name,
  • e-mail address and phone number,
  • the description of the order and any documents you attach,
  • whether you are interested in substitute fulfilment.

Why and on what legal basis

We use the data to handle your request, prepare a quote and agree the order with you. The legal basis is taking steps towards a contract and performing it under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

If an order is placed, we also keep the data from invoices and other documents because accounting and tax law requires us to (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not sell your data or use it to send advertising.

How long we keep the data

Requests that do not lead to an order are kept for no longer than 1 year after our last contact. Data about completed orders is kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, at most 10 years.

Who we share the data with

The request form is sent through Google Apps Script and delivered to us by e-mail via Gmail, so Google processes the data as our processor. Some of the processing may take place outside the EU, based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps technical logs of every visit, such as the IP address, for security and to run the service.

Cookies

Our website itself does not use any cookies for tracking or advertising. It has no analytics tools or advertising code, and the font is stored on our own website.

The only thing we store in your browser is your choice from the cookie bar, so the website does not ask you again. This choice is kept for 12 months and is necessary for the website to work.

The Google map (Street View) on the Contact page stores Google cookies. That is why we only load it when you agree in the cookie bar or click the Show map button. How Google handles the data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com.

You can change your choice or withdraw your consent at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page.

The links to Google Maps, Apple Maps and Waze open websites of other companies, which have their own cookie rules.

Your rights

With regard to your personal data, you have the right to:

  • get information about what data we process about you, and a copy of it,
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data,
  • ask us to erase the data or restrict its processing,
  • object to the processing, and to data portability,
  • withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Send your requests to info@invako.cz. If you believe we are not handling your data correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.gov.cz).

This policy applies from 23 November 2026.
