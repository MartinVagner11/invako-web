Sheltered workshop

We employ people with disabilities – and Czech companies can use our services towards their quota.

Quality production that helps

INVAKO is a sheltered workshop – we employ people with disabilities and give them meaningful work in a technical field.

We make no compromises on quality: we work on modern CNC machines and every part passes a final inspection before shipping.

Meet your quota with substitute fulfilment

Under Czech law, companies with more than 25 employees must employ 4 % people with disabilities. By buying our products and services you can meet this obligation through substitute fulfilment – and instead of paying a levy to the state, you get parts you actually need.
