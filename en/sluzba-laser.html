Laser cutting and marking

Laser cutting and marking

Technology with high precision and attention to detail allows us to create perfectly smooth cut edges without the need for further finishing and to apply permanent marking to a wide range of materials.

Applications

  • Promotional items
  • Wayfinding systems
  • Labels
  • Jewellery
  • Personalised gifts

Main advantages

  • Laser cutting: Perfectly clean, glossy edges (especially on plexiglass/acrylic) without burrs. Ideal for plexiglass, wood, paper, leather or textiles.
  • Laser marking and engraving: Permanent, detailed text, logos or graphics on metals, plastics, wood, glass or leather.
