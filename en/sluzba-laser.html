Laser cutting and marking

Laser cutting and marking

Laser cutting and engraving with fine detail. Modern laser technology lets us create perfectly smooth cut edges without any further finishing and apply permanent markings to a wide range of materials.

Applications

  • Promotional items
  • Wayfinding systems
  • Labels
  • Jewellery
  • Personalised gifts

Main advantages

  • Laser cutting: Perfectly clean, glossy edges (especially on plexiglass/acrylic) without burrs. Ideal for plexiglass, wood, paper, leather or textiles.
  • Laser marking and engraving: Permanent, detailed text, logos or graphics on metals, plastics, wood, glass or leather.
